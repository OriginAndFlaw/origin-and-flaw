This looks like a rock. It is actually a two thousand year old computer.
In 1901, sponge divers pulled it from a Roman-era shipwreck off a tiny Greek island. For two years nobody looked at it twice.
Then someone noticed a gear wheel inside.
X-rays and CT scans revealed thirty bronze gears. Turn a crank, and it showed the Sun, the Moon, the phase of the Moon, and when the next eclipse would come.
Nothing this complex appears again for over a thousand years.
