Two boys are riding their bicycles on an old, empty runway in Canada.
Nearby, families are at a weekend car race.
Then they see it. A huge passenger jet, coming straight at them.
With no engine sound at all.
The jet had run out of fuel at forty one thousand feet.
The crew had glided it here, to an old air force runway at Gimli.
The boys rode for their lives.
The nose wheel collapsed, and the nose scraped along the concrete, slowing the jet down.
It stopped before it reached them.
Nobody on the ground was hurt.
