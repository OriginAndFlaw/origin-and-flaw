Forty one thousand feet. Sixty nine people. And both engines just stopped.
This jet had run out of fuel. Not because of a leak. Because of one number.
Canada was switching to metric. The fuel was calculated in pounds instead of kilograms.
The maths was perfect. The unit was wrong. The jet took off with less than half the fuel it needed.
Now the pilots had no engines, almost no instruments, and an old air force runway with a car race on it.
