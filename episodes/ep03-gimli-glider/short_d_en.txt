In 1983, Canada was switching from pounds to kilograms.
Road signs changed. Supermarket labels changed.
And a brand new Boeing 767 measured its fuel in kilograms.
But the fuel was worked out with a number meant for pounds.
A pound is less than half a kilogram.
So the jet took off with less than half the fuel it needed.
And its fuel gauges were not working that day, so nobody saw it.
At forty one thousand feet, both engines stopped.
One number. The wrong unit.
