Look up. This is the Pantheon in Rome.
It is still the largest dome in the world made of concrete with no steel inside it.
No steel bars. No modern cement. And it has stood for almost nineteen hundred years.
How?
The Romans changed the recipe as the dome rose.
Heavy stone at the bottom.
Lighter and lighter material higher up.
Near the top, pumice: a volcanic rock so full of air bubbles that it can float on water.
A dome that gets lighter as it climbs.
The full story of Roman concrete is on the channel.
