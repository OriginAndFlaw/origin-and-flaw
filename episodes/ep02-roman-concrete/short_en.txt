This concrete is two thousand years old. And it can heal its own cracks.
For a century, scientists saw tiny white lumps inside Roman concrete and called them mistakes.
In 2023, a team at MIT found they were not mistakes at all.
They were pockets of lime, made by mixing it hot.
When a crack lets water in, that lime dissolves, turns into new mineral, and seals the crack shut.
Their test cracks closed in about two weeks.
