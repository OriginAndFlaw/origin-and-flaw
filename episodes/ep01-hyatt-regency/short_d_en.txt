July seventeenth, 1981. Kansas City.
About sixteen hundred people are dancing in the new Hyatt Regency hotel.
People are standing on the walkways above, watching the band.
At five past seven, there is a loud crack.
Two walkways fall into the crowd below.
A hundred and fourteen people never went home.
It was the deadliest structural collapse in American history at that time.
The cause was not a bomb. Not an earthquake.
It was one small change to how two steel rods were hung.
And nobody checked the maths.
