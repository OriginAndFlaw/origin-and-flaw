Every engineering student in the world studies this one nut.
Here is why.
The plan said one long steel rod would hold two walkways, one above the other.
Each nut carried one walkway.
Then the plan changed to two shorter rods.
It looked almost the same on paper.
But now the top nut carried the top walkway and the bottom walkway too.
Double the load. On a nut sitting on a weak welded seam.
In July 1981, that nut pulled straight through the steel.
A hundred and fourteen people died.
Same steel. Same nut. One small change.
