Two walkways hung one above the other. One long steel rod held both.
Then someone asked for a small change. Two short rods instead of one.
It looked the same on paper. It was not.
Now the upper walkway carried the lower one too. The load on one nut doubled.
That nut sat on a welded seam in a hollow steel beam.
July seventeenth, nineteen eighty one. Sixteen hundred people dancing below.
At five past seven, that nut pulled straight through the steel.
