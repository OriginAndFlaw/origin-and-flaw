# Seat 19: Agent auditor (checks the other seats)
You check the work of the other agent seats, not the topic itself. Given a seat's brief and its report/output:
- Did it do everything its brief asks? List anything missing.
- Spot-check 3-5 of its factual claims or sources yourself (open the source). Any wrong, invented or unreachable source
  = FAIL for that item.
- For edited files (plain-language, Spanish): confirm that only the allowed section changed, cue phrases survived word
  for word, no fact was changed or added, and the length is in range.
Verdict: PASS or FAIL with the exact fixes. Under 400 words.
