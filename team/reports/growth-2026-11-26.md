# Seat 18 growth report, 26 Nov 2026 (channel plan + EP06 check)

## 1. Channel: fastest honest route to 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours

**The deadline matters more than any lever.** From 1 Feb 2027 new YPP applicants need 8,000 public watch hours in 365 days (or 20M Shorts views in 90 days), not 4,000. 1,000 subscribers stays. It is not yet published how applications pending on that date are treated. Shorts watch time, unlisted, private and ad-campaign views never count.
- 4,000 h by 31 Jan = about 60 hours a day from today. For a new channel that is only possible if one video breaks out. **So plan for 8,000 h in 12 months** and treat 4,000 before the deadline as a bonus.
- The maths: a 10-min episode at ~35% retention is ~3.5 min a view, so 8,000 h ≈ 137,000 long-form views. Spread over ~180 episodes in a year that is about **760 views per episode** across its life. Raising average view time to 5 min cuts the need to ~96,000 views (-30%). **Retention is the biggest lever.**

| Lever | Likely effect (estimate) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Search titles on evergreen topics ("Tacoma Narrows", "Galloping Gertie") | the largest steady source for a small channel; search views keep coming for years | small channels rarely get browse traffic; search does not depend on subscribers |
| Retention: cut background, re-hook every 60-90 s | +1-1.5 min a view = -25-30% views needed | the table above |
| Pillar playlists ("The 1-Inch Mistake") as series, set to auto-play in order | +10-20% session watch time | binge viewers play the next episode without choosing |
| End screen: "best for viewer" + the playlist, last 20 s | 1-5% of viewers continue | needs an end that does not feel like the end; keep the teaser after the CTA |
| Compilations (EP01-06 as a 45-60 min "Engineering mistakes" cut), once 6+ episodes exist | each view is worth 10-20 min, not 3.5 | sleep/background viewers watch long cuts; built from real episodes, not filler, so not "inauthentic" |
| Shorts "Related video" link to the long episode, plus a spoken CTA | about 1-3% of Short viewers click (one public test saw ~10% with a strong CTA) | Shorts bring subscribers; watch hours come only from this click |
| Spanish audio track | extra watch time from Spanish viewers on the same video, no new upload | multi-language audio adds to the same video's public hours |
| Pinned comment with the yes/no question; Community poll the day before release | more comments in the first hour, a small push in how widely it is shown | cheap; do it every time |
| Premieres | small until about 1,000 subs | the countdown counts only if people show up |
| Posting time | long video 14:00-15:00 UTC (9-10 ET, 14-15 UK); Short 19:30 UTC is fine | indexed before the US/UK evening peak |

Subscribers: Shorts are the subscriber engine (one Short a day is right). Add a one-line subscribe reason in each Short's last 2 s only when the Short has paid off.

## 2. EP06 Tacoma Narrows: scores

**Topic warning:** Tacoma and "it wasn't resonance" are already well covered on YouTube. The playbook says to use an unknown angle or skip. The fresh material here is the people (riders, lemons, Tubby, the 16 fps film). Lead with that, not the myth.

| Part | Score | The one change |
|---|---|---|
| Title | 7 | Add the search term: **"Galloping Gertie: The Bridge Your Textbook Got Wrong"** (52). Title 1 is the best for clicks but nobody searches it; use it as an A/B test variant. |
| Thumbnail | 6 | A repeats the title and B uses jargon. Use the deck tilted steeply with one tiny car, text **"45°"** (one number) |
| First 15 s | 7 | Good, it starts inside the strange moment. Cut "Today I want to show you..." to one line so the "your textbook is wrong" promise lands before 0:20 |
| Short | 7 | Open on the twist clip with "Your physics teacher got this wrong." Give it its own title (it currently copies the long title). The Arabic text is empty. |

**Shareability:** "the only death was a dog, and the professor who tried to save him got bitten" (~5:00) and "the famous film plays too fast" (~6:10). Both are strong but come late. Tease the film-speed detail in the hook ("even the famous film is lying to you").

**Watch-time risks (section 3, 1,566 words ≈ 10 min, OK)**
1. **0:45-1:15, "In the late 1930s, Washington State wanted..."**: classic background drop. Open the chapter with the conflict: "The safe design lost over three million dollars."
2. **~5:15, the subscribe line in the middle of the collapse** breaks the climax. Move it to just before "So why did the bridge twist" (~6:35): same rule, a better seam.
3. **6:40-7:30, flutter**: abstract. Put the "once a second vs once every five seconds" numbers on screen as callouts, and show the swing and flag diagrams side by side.
4. **8:20-9:30, the after-story**: energy drops. Shorten the definitions (wind tunnel, scale model, Register) to one clause each, and reach the reef ("it is now a reef") sooner as the last surprise.
5. End: keep the teaser after the question. Point the end screen at the "1-Inch Mistake" playlist; the next episode is not live yet.
