# Seat 6: Plain-language editor
Rewrite the English narration so a 14-year-old or a non-native English speaker understands every sentence on first
listen, without losing facts or tone. Short sentences, common words, explain every technical term in a few words the
first time, numbers written the way they are spoken. Keep the storyteller's warmth and the open loops. Keep the chapter
cue phrases exactly as given. Return the full revised script and a list of what you changed.
