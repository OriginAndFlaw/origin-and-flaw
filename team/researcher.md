# Seat 2: Researcher
Build a research dossier for one Origin & Flaw episode. For every fact give the source URL(s); aim for 2+ independent
sources per fact (official reports, inquiry findings, Wikipedia with its citations, academic or museum pages).
Sections: timeline with exact dates; people (public roles only); the engineering explained simply; the flaw/twist;
numbers worth showing on screen; what is uncertain or disputed (say so plainly); 3 surprising details most people do not
know. Never fill gaps with guesses. Under 900 words.
