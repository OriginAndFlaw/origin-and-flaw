# Origin & Flaw: episode playbook

Goal: 1,000,000 subscribers. Every episode must be good enough to stand next to the best documentary
channels on YouTube, and must never look mass-produced (YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule blocks
monetization for templated AI slideshows).

## Topic
- Evergreen, surprising, with a real flaw at its heart: ancient engineering, the secret history of everyday
  objects, accidental inventions, "the 1-inch mistake". No politics, religion or current affairs.
- Test the title first: would a curious stranger in the US or UK click it? Under 60 characters, honest,
  a specific number or an impossible-sounding fact ("Your Frying Pan Helped Build the Atomic Bomb").
- Facts from at least one solid source per claim; drop anything the sources disagree on.

## Script (English 1,500-1,700 words = 9-11 min with the Gemini voice; never under 8 min)
- 0-15 s: the hook. Start inside the strangest moment, not with background. Promise what the viewer
  will understand by the end.
- 15-45 s: why it matters to the viewer personally ("you probably used it today").
- A re-hook every 60-90 s: an open question, a "hold on to that number", a reversal.
- Talk like a person: "When I first saw...", "Here is the part that still gives me chills", "you".
- One subscribe line, under 5 seconds, just before the biggest reveal.
- The flaw chapter near the end: the twist that gives the channel its name.
- End: one question for the comments + a one-line teaser of the next episode (it must be delivered).
- Each episode opens and is built differently from the last (cold open, countdown, mystery object,
  timeline...), so no two feel templated.

## Picture
- 50-60 stills for a 10-minute episode (one every 8-10 s); the render also splits any hold over 7 s
  into closer shots.
- 4 Flow hook clips + 1-2 mid-episode clips at the peak moments (Omni 1.1 Flash 720p 4 s).
- A diagram, map or timeline wherever the script explains something technical.
- Number callouts (episode.json "callouts") for every key number the narration says.
- No real people's faces, no logos, no readable text in AI images, no gore.

## Sound
- Narrator: Gemini voice Charon, recorded on the PC (OAF_SAVE_VOICE=1) and committed in episodes/<ep>/voice.
- Spanish audio track (script written by hand, not machine-translated) for YouTube's multi-language audio.
- Owner's Suno music, ducked under the voice.

## Shorts
- One companion Short + three extra Shorts per episode, each with its own hook and angle; one post a day.

## After posting
- Watch retention: where viewers leave, fix that pattern in the next script.
- Answer comments; use good questions as future topics.
